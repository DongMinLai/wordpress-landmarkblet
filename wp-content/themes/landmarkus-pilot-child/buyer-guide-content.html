Order basics

What is the minimum wholesale belt order?

Wholesale belt orders start at 12 belts in total. You can mix styles across that order; the minimum is not 12 of each style. Choose from the options listed on each product page.

Who is the wholesale catalog for?

The wholesale catalog is for retailers, online sellers, distributors, and other business buyers sourcing belts. Start with the styles that suit your store, then review their available options.

Are prices per belt or per dozen?

Wholesale belt prices are per belt, not per dozen. Enter the number of individual belts you want for each selected option.

Styles, colors & sizes

How can I find a style or reorder by SKU?

Browse the wholesale belt catalog by category, or search using the SKU from a previous order. Color and size filters help narrow the list. Check the current product options before reordering.

How do I choose a color and size?

On the product page, use the color photos where available and choose a listed size. Check the selected color and size before entering quantities. Quick view is useful for browsing; open the product page to build your order.

How should I read the size labels?

Use the measurements and size labels on the specific product page. A waist range and an overall belt length describe different measurements; do not assume every style uses the same sizing.

If a label is unclear, email us the SKU and the size you are considering before ordering.

Building & checking your order

How do I add several product options at once?

On a product page with a Bulk order section, enter a quantity next to each option you want. You can also select rows and apply the same quantity to those selected rows.

Review the quantities, then select Add quantities to cart. Only the options listed for that product are available to order.

How do I know my items were added?

Wait for the add-to-cart confirmation, then select View cart. Check the SKUs, colors, sizes, and quantities there before continuing. If an error appears, follow the message and check your cart before submitting again.

Which payment options can I use?

The payment options available for your order are shown at checkout. If you need a payment option that is not listed, contact us before placing the order.

How do I confirm shipping costs and delivery timing?

Contact us with the product SKUs, quantities, and delivery ZIP code to confirm shipping availability, cost, and timing before placing your order. Include the date you need the belts if you are buying for an event or a planned restock.

Samples, returns & custom requests

Can I ask about samples before a larger order?

Yes. Contact us about samples for the styles you are considering. Include the SKUs and delivery destination, and confirm sample availability, charges, and delivery arrangements before ordering.

What should I do if there is an issue with my order?

Contact us with your order number, the affected SKUs, and a description of the issue. For a return request, confirm eligibility and the return arrangements with us before sending items back.

What if I need a logo or a custom belt?

Custom projects are handled separately from a wholesale catalog order. Send the style reference, requested changes, quantities, and destination so we can discuss your requirements.

The 12-belt wholesale minimum does not define the minimum for a custom project. Custom quantities, samples, pricing, and timing need to be confirmed for that project.

Buying support

Have a question about a specific style?

Send the SKU, the options and quantities you have in mind, and your delivery ZIP code. We can use those details to discuss your order.
